The Realm Divided

Axentia MMXXVI

Sixteen great houses hold sixteen disciplines, and each keeps its own seat somewhere in these lands. The map below is dark and it will stay dark. Descend, take the reins in your hand, and drag the light across the realm — every tower you want to see, you must find.

Descend VI & VII August MMXXVI · Bangalore Central Campus
Chapter the First · The Painted Table

A house is a sigil and a set of words

The sigil is what the realm sees from a distance — the mark, the accent, the thing that makes a house unmistakable. The words are what remains once the banners come down. Axentia has always been both: Accent and Essentia. Two days will decide which of the two each house truly possesses.

Chapter the Second · The Realm Rises

Nothing is shown to the idle hand

Below lies a painted table under a shroud of mist. Press and hold until the realm answers, then drag: a lantern moves with your hand, and only what it passes over wakes. Winterfell's grey walls, the Rock's gilded crown, the Eyrie's pale needles, the great beacon of Oldtown — sixteen holds, and not one of them will come to you. No house inherits its standing. It is found.

Chapter the Third · Nightfall

Sixteen fires against the dark

Every hold you uncover raises its towers out of the map, hangs its banner in the wind, and lights its arrow slits one by one. Something older wakes with the dark, too — look up, and you will find wings crossing the realm. They mark the hand that moves over the map; reach toward one, and it may answer in fire. This is the hour the map stops being a picture and becomes a choice.

Set Your Hand to It

The Oath Roll

For a sworn company, the leader alone sets the seal — but every companion must be named upon the roll. One house per knight: the moment a name is sealed to a house, that name is refused by every other. This holds for companions as much as for leaders.

By setting your hand to this roll you accept the laws of the realm. The roll closes III August MMXXVI, 23:59 IST.

The Roll Refuses This Company

    A knight may ride under one house only. Remove or replace the named companion, or have them withdraw from their first house through the coordinator.
